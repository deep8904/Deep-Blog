# Opening

Start with a concrete observation, moment, or problem. Avoid a generic thesis paragraph.

## Section title

Develop one idea at a time. Use short paragraphs, specific examples, and honest uncertainty.

> Use blockquotes only when a sentence genuinely deserves to be isolated.

## Closing

End with a changed understanding, a useful question, or a decision—not a forced summary.

# Content strategy and website voice

## What changed

The previous copy sounded like an art-direction statement. The revised copy sounds like a person introducing his own blog.

## Core introduction

Deep is a software engineer whose interests naturally extend into interface design, game development, and photography. Draft State is a place to record what those activities teach him: decisions, mistakes, observations, and ideas worth revisiting.

## Voice

- First person.
- Direct and conversational.
- Specific about the work.
- Curious rather than self-important.
- Honest about uncertainty.
- Edited, but not corporate.

## Homepage structure

1. **Who is this?** Deep, a software engineer with overlapping interests.
2. **Why does he write?** To understand the work and preserve its lessons.
3. **What will appear here?** Software, interfaces, games, and photography.
4. **What is published now?** Nothing yet; the first essay is in progress.
5. **Where should a reader go next?** Notes or About.

## Writing guidance for future posts

A strong post should usually begin with something concrete:
- A decision made during a build.
- A failed prototype.
- An interface that caused confusion.
- A game system that changed the experience.
- A photograph or event that revealed a useful idea.

Then explain:
- What happened.
- Why it mattered.
- What changed after closer examination.
- What remains uncertain.

Avoid generic openings, forced listicles, exaggerated expertise, and a publishing schedule created only to keep the archive busy.
